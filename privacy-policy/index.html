Privacy Policy

This basic privacy policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Aikyam Rise Club public website or contact the club.

1. Information We May Collect

If you contact the club, we may receive information such as your name, phone number, email address or other details that you choose to provide.

The public website is primarily informational. We do not require an account to browse the main website.

2. How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided to us may be used to respond to enquiries, provide information about club activities, membership or partnership opportunities, and communicate about relevant programs.

3. Member Portal

The Member Login link takes you to a separate member portal at member.aikyamriseclub.com. Information submitted through that portal may be subject to additional terms and privacy practices presented there.

4. Cookies and Analytics

The website may use basic technical cookies or third-party services for security, performance or usage measurement. Where applicable, those services may process limited technical information such as browser or device data.

5. Third-Party Services

Links to third-party websites, social media platforms or the member portal are governed by the privacy practices of those third parties.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information voluntarily shared with us and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided or as required by law.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests regarding information you have shared with the club, please contact the club using the contact details published on this website.
