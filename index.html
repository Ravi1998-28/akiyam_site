About the Club

Unity that helps young people rise.

“Aikyam” is derived from Sanskrit, meaning oneness or unity. The club embodies togetherness through sports, culture and community.

“Rise” represents upliftment — a call to move beyond stagnation and mediocrity, helping young people become aware of their potential and purpose.
